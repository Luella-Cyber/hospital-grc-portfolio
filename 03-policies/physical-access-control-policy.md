This is a sample policy for a hypothetical hospital, written for a portfolio case study. It does not describe any real facility's policy.

## 1. Purpose

To make sure only authorized people can enter hospital buildings and patient areas, and that keys, badges, and doors are managed so that access can be traced and removed quickly.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers all employees, contractors, and vendors, and every door, lock, key, badge, and badge reader that controls access to hospital buildings and patient areas.

## 3. Policy statements

1. **Access by role.** Access to patient areas is granted according to job role. Each request is approved by the person's manager and the Security Director.
2. **Keys.** Keys are issued only to authorized staff, signed for when issued, and recorded in a key log. Master keys are kept in a locked, access-controlled location.
3. **Key and badge inventory.** The key log and the list of active badges are checked against actual holders every quarter. Missing items are reported the same shift.
4. **Lost or stolen items.** Lost, stolen, or unreturned keys and badges are reported immediately. A lost badge is deactivated right away. The Security Director decides whether locks must be re-keyed.
5. **Badge use.** Badges are worn visibly, are never lent or shared, and are never copied.
6. **Doors.** Doors to patient areas stay closed and locked. Staff do not prop them open. A door held open too long triggers an alarm that security responds to.
7. **Tailgating.** Each person uses their own badge to enter a controlled door. Staff politely challenge anyone following them in without a badge and report the person to security if needed.
8. **Joiners, movers, and leavers.** Badges are issued when a person starts, updated when their role changes, and deactivated on or before their last day. For involuntary departures, access is removed the same day, and all keys and badges are collected.
9. **Contractors and vendors.** Contractors receive time-limited badges that expire on the end date of the work. They are escorted in patient areas unless the Security Director has approved otherwise.
10. **Access reviews.** Managers review who has access to patient areas every quarter. Access that is no longer needed is removed within 5 business days.
11. **Badge and door logs.** Logs from the badge system are reviewed monthly for unusual patterns, such as entry at odd hours, repeated denied attempts, or one badge used in two places. Findings go to the Security Director. Logs are kept according to the records retention schedule.
12. **Emergency entry.** A written procedure explains how authorized staff may enter a locked area in an emergency. Each use is logged and reviewed afterward.
13. **Maintenance records.** Repairs and changes to doors, locks, and badge readers are recorded, including the date, the work done, and who did it.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Security Director | Owns this policy, approves access to patient areas, and approves exceptions |
| Facilities Manager | Maintains doors, locks, and readers, keeps repair records, and manages re-keying |
| HR and IT Security | Create, change, and remove badge access when people join, move, or leave |
| Managers | Approve access requests and complete quarterly access reviews |
| All staff | Protect their keys and badges, report losses, and challenge unbadged people |

## 5. Exceptions

Exceptions need written approval from the Security Director, a stated reason, and an end date.

## 6. Enforcement

Violations may lead to loss of access and disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.

## 7. Review

The Security Director reviews this policy once a year and after any significant incident.
