This is a sample policy for a hypothetical hospital, written for a portfolio case study. It does not describe any real facility's policy.

## 1. Purpose

To control who enters hospital buildings and patient areas, keep prohibited items out, and protect patients, staff, and information.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all visitors, including family members, approved special visitors, delivery personnel, and contractors, and to the staff who host or screen them.

## 3. Policy statements

1. **Check-in.** Every visitor checks in at the front desk, shows government-issued photo identification, and signs the visitor log with the time of arrival.
2. **Approved visitors only.** Visitors enter patient areas only if they are on a patient's approved list or have been approved in advance by the responsible clinical staff.
3. **Screening.** Before entering a patient area, visitors are screened using the methods approved by hospital leadership, including a check of belongings.
4. **Prohibited items.** A list of prohibited items is posted at check-in and shared with visitors in advance. Prohibited items are stored or returned at check-in and are never taken into patient areas.
5. **Visitor badges.** Each visitor receives a temporary badge, wears it visibly, and returns it when leaving. The time out is recorded in the visitor log.
6. **Escorts.** Visitors are escorted in any area that is not open to the public, unless a supervisor has approved otherwise.
7. **Special visits.** Visits by legal representatives, clergy, advocates, and other special visitors are arranged in advance, approved by a supervisor and clinical staff, and supervised as required.
8. **Deliveries and contractors.** Delivery staff and contractors follow the same check-in. Contractors working in patient areas are escorted for the full visit.
9. **Challenging unbadged people.** Staff politely ask anyone without a badge in a restricted area to return to the front desk, and report the person to security if they do not.
10. **Stopping a visit.** Security or clinical staff may end a visit at any time if there is a safety or policy concern.
11. **Records.** Visitor logs are kept according to the hospital's records retention schedule and are available for review after an incident.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Security Director | Owns this policy, approves screening methods, and approves exceptions |
| Front desk and security staff | Check in and screen visitors, issue and collect badges, and keep the log |
| Clinical staff | Approve visits to patient areas and supervise special visits |
| All staff | Challenge unbadged people and report concerns |

## 5. Exceptions

Exceptions need written approval from the Security Director, a stated reason, and an end date.

## 6. Enforcement

Violations by staff may lead to retraining or disciplinary action. Visitors who break the rules may lose visiting privileges.

## 7. Review

The Security Director reviews this policy once a year and after any significant incident.
